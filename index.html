About

A collection of urban boulder problems (builders) in and around Burlington, Vermont.

Most spots are best at night, but I've never been bothered. Use your best judgement, if you are asked to leave, please listen (and let me know!) If you try any of these climbs, or have anything to add, please also let me know! Feel free to email me lucydrur@gmail.com.
